## Who this is for

Anyone who ends up in the incident channel: engineers, managers, support leads. No infrastructure knowledge required.

## What you will build

You command three simulated incidents of increasing chaos, write the postmortem for each, and leave with a severity matrix and an incident playbook template ready for your team.
