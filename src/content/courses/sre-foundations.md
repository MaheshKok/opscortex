## Who this is for

Backend engineers, ops folks and new SREs who want the discipline behind the title. No prior SRE experience required. Production experience strongly recommended.

## What you will build

A complete SLO framework for a sample service: SLIs, recording rules, burn-rate alerts, an error-budget policy document and an on-call rotation with a handoff checklist.
